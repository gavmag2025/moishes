About Moishes

A neighbourhood Mehadrin butchery and deli in Glenhazel.

Our story

Moishes Butchery & Deli serves the Glenhazel and Raedene community with fresh kosher meat and poultry, a deli counter, challah and baked goods, cooked chicken, kugels, packed meals and catering. Everything is cut to order and packed for Shabbos.

This website is a demonstration built for the shop. All products, prices, hours and kashrut details shown are dummy data. Always confirm with the shop.
